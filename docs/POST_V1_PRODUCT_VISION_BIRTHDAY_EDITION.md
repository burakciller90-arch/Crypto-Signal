# POST-V1 PRODUCT VISION — BIRTHDAY EDITION

Status: governing product intent for the focused V2+ release.
Decision date: 2026-09-20.
REAL_CAPITAL=0 remains mandatory.

## Why this exists
The user is building Crypto Signal as a birthday gift for a friend and wants the result to feel like a serious,
world-class market-intelligence product rather than a developer dashboard or a generic BUY/SELL bot.
V1 is technically accepted, but V1 acceptance is the foundation, not the final product experience.

## Product thesis
World-class does not mean maximum feature count.
The release should be deliberately narrow, trustworthy, fast to understand, visually premium and genuinely useful.
A user should be able to open the product and understand within roughly 20-30 seconds:
- what in the monitored market deserves attention,
- why it deserves attention,
- which methodologies agree or disagree,
- what would invalidate the idea,
- what important uncertainty remains,
- and what the platform has actually learned from immutable historical evidence.

## What must remain true
- Data Truth, PIT safety, closed-candle truth and immutable evidence are never weakened for presentation.
- PA/SMC/ICT, Harmonic and Elliott stay independently testable.
- Confluence remains an agreement index, not a probability.
- No fabricated win rate, confidence or calibrated probability.
- LIVE_UNTOUCHED_FORWARD, WALK_FORWARD and RETROSPECTIVE evidence remain distinct.
- Losses, ambiguity and NOT_EVALUABLE outcomes remain visible.
- Higher-timeframe truth comes from canonical closed 15m aggregation.
- LLM text may explain evidence but never create numeric market truth.
- No order placement and REAL_CAPITAL remains 0.

## Turkish-first presentation
The domain contracts may remain stable English identifiers internally, while the product presentation is Turkish-first.
Preferred user-facing language includes:
- Command Center -> Komuta Merkezi
- Market Radar -> Piyasa Radarı
- Asset Cockpit -> Varlık Merkezi
- Signal Detail -> Sinyal Detayı
- Signal Archive -> Sinyal Arşivi
- Performance -> Performans
- Alert Center -> Uyarılar
- WATCH -> İzleniyor
- ACTIVE -> Aktif Sinyal
- NEUTRAL -> Nötr
- INVALIDATED -> Geçersizleşti
- Confluence -> Metodoloji Uyumu
- Uncertainty -> Belirsizlikler
Technical English terms may be retained where they are market-standard, with clear Turkish explanation.

## Target experience
The home surface should lead with useful intelligence, not database telemetry.
It should summarize the monitored universe, surface the few most important situations, then allow drill-down.
A high-value setup view should show direction, timeframe, methodology votes, evidence summary, uncertainty,
frozen geometry when present, invalidation and targets when scientifically available, and relevant multi-timeframe context.
Charts should make backend intelligence visible: structure, BOS/CHoCH, liquidity, FVG/BPR, Harmonic geometry,
Elliott candidates and important levels where supported by frozen evidence.

## Explicit non-goals for the Birthday Edition
Do not expand into a Bloomberg clone.
Do not block the release on autonomous trading, broad social/news intelligence, deep order-book microstructure,
large ML research programs, or unsupported probability models.
Those may follow only after the focused product is excellent and sufficient evidence exists.

## Product quality bar
A release is not world-class merely because tests pass.
It must satisfy all three:
1. Scientific integrity — no false certainty or hindsight.
2. Coverage — materially reduces manual chart chasing.
3. Product usefulness — the important market state is obvious quickly and drill-down is understandable.

V1 is strong on scientific integrity.
The focused V2+ program primarily raises coverage and product usefulness without sacrificing V1 truth.
