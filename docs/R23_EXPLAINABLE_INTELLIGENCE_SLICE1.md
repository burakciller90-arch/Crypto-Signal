## Locked product rule

SIMPLE and PRO are two projections of the **same immutable Decision Proof**.
SIMPLE may translate mechanics into beginner language, but it cannot:

- invent evidence;
- upgrade INSUFFICIENT/UNSUPPORTED to confirmation;
- turn a contradiction into support;
- convert Confluence score into probability;
- show calibrated probability unless R19-backed probability exists in the proof;
- hide uncertainty or event risk;
- expose private chain-of-thought;
- create execution or capital authority.

Every SIMPLE evidence line is bound to the exact same slice identity,
availability and verdict as its PRO counterpart.

## SIMPLE

SIMPLE uses deterministic plain-language domain labels:

- frozen chart evidence;
- price candles;
- order-book pressure;
- nearby liquidity;
- liquidation pressure;
- buyer/seller flow;
- futures positioning;
- on-chain activity;
- event risk;
- price-action methodology;
- probability calibration.

Wording is deliberately bounded:

- SUPPORT -> “supports the current setup”;
- CONTRADICT -> “is working against the current setup”;
- NEUTRAL -> “is not giving a directional confirmation”;
- INSUFFICIENT/UNSUPPORTED -> “does not have enough accepted evidence yet”.

The overall summary is capped by the technical evidence state:
\`INCOMPLETE\`, \`CONFLICTED\`, \`CONDITIONAL\`, or
\`CALIBRATED_CONDITIONAL\`. None of these means certainty.

## PRO

PRO exposes the accepted technical surface and exact provenance:

- order-book imbalance;
- liquidity / liquidations;
- CVD / delta / absorption;
- OI / funding / basis;
- event risk;
- PA / methodology;
- probability-calibration status;
- evidence identities;
- source quality;
- market-available timestamp;
- observed timestamp;
- freshness;
- summary codes;
- exact availability and verdict.

When a numeric measurement is not present in the accepted Decision Proof,
R23 says so explicitly. It does **not** manufacture a number from an evidence ID.

## Probability and uncertainty

Confluence support/opposition remains methodology evidence, not win probability.
If \`calibrated_probability_0_1\` is absent, both views state that probability
is not calibrated for the forecast scope.

If calibrated R19 evidence is present, R23 may display that exact calibrated
probability for the exact proof scope and no other inferred confidence.
