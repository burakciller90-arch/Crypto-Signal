# INTELLIGENCE & ALPHA FACTORY ARCHITECTURE

Status: governing research architecture for Stage 8+.
REAL_CAPITAL=0.

## Principle
Crypto Signal may learn and may propose new strategies. It may not silently rewrite the production decision engine or self-promote a strategy.

## Independent evidence engines
Core: Price Action/SMC/ICT, Harmonic, Elliott.
Expansion candidates: trend/momentum, mean reversion, breakout/volatility, order flow/microstructure, derivatives/leverage, on-chain/network, sentiment/attention and cross-market context.

## Regime-aware weighting
A regime engine identifies market conditions. Method weights may vary by regime only through an accepted, versioned policy.

## Meta decision
The meta layer must avoid double-counting correlated signals. Contradiction and abstention are first-class. "No trade" is valid.

## Alpha Factory
The research environment can generate hypotheses from symbolic rules, feature interactions, tree models, clustering, evolutionary search and later bounded ML/RL.

## Champion/challenger gate
A challenger must pass:
1. data-contract and leakage audit,
2. deterministic/reproducible training or rule generation,
3. transaction-cost/slippage stress,
4. in-sample sanity,
5. out-of-sample evaluation,
6. walk-forward evaluation,
7. untouched-forward paper evaluation,
8. robustness/ablation checks,
9. supervisor acceptance.

No single backtest result can promote a challenger.

## Learning memory
Persist what worked, where, when, under which version and regime. Persist failures equally. Learning is evidence, not authority.

## Probability policy
No probability label until accepted calibration evidence exists. Agreement, confidence heuristics and historical frequency remain separately named concepts.
