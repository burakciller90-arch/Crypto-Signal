# BEGINNER UX, EVIDENCE CENTER & LIVE EXPERIENCE SPEC

Status: governing UX specification for Stages 6A, 6B and 9.

## Product promise
A person who knows nothing about crypto should be able to understand what the system sees, why it matters, what could invalidate it and what the virtual portfolio would do.

## Auto refresh
The user must not need F5.
The UI must refresh itself while preserving navigation state. It must show connection health, last update time and stale-data state.

## Signal explanation hierarchy
Every signal detail should answer in this order:
1. Sistem ne görüyor?
2. Neden önemli?
3. Neden şimdi?
4. Hangi metodlar destekliyor?
5. Hangi metodlar karşı çıkıyor veya belirsiz?
6. Grafik kanıtı nerede?
7. Bu fikir ne zaman bozulur?
8. Neden işlem yapmamalıyız?
9. Sanal portföy olsaydı ne yapardı?
10. Kullanıcı bunu öğrenmek isterse kavram açıklaması.

## "Bana Öğret"
Definitions must be short, Turkish-first and contextual to the currently visible chart.
Beginner mode avoids jargon. Advanced mode may expose deeper methodology detail.

## Evidence rendering
Only frozen evidence may be drawn as decision proof. Newer/live price may be shown separately and must never masquerade as decision-time evidence.

## Virtual purchase plan
Show:
- virtual amount,
- asset/side,
- simulated entry/fill,
- fee/spread/slippage,
- resulting cash/exposure,
- invalidation/risk,
- rationale,
- counter-case.

## Clarity rule
More detail must come from progressive disclosure, not visual clutter.
